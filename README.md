# Luvuno-s-Farm-Business-Analysis-System-Proposal

##  Project Overview
This project aims to help a livestock farm improve operational efficiency by developing a web-based system that enables customers to place orders and make payments online. Customers will also have the ability to cancel orders and track the status of their purchases. On the management side, the farm owner can monitor incoming orders and receive weekly or monthly reports on sales performance and customer retention.

## Problem Statement
The farm relied on manual processes. This led to inefficiencies within the farm as there was no system to manage the orders, customers, and products/inventories. 

## My Role
- As a business analyst my role was to analyse the business processes, identify the stakeholders, gather and identify business requirements, and structure the database components.
